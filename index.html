About Me

I am a faculty member at Nova Scotia Community College at the Center of Geographic Science (COGS) with a background in software development, GIS programming, web application development, Linux administration, automation, and cloud technologies. Before teaching, I worked extensively with ESRI technologies, Leaflet, and web-based GIS applications. Today, I focus on helping students develop practical skills that prepare them for industry and lifelong learning.


Department: Information Technology in Programming (ITPR) of the School of Applied Technology and Innovation

Campus: Centre of Geographic Sciences (COGS) of the Annapolis Valley Campus

Teaching Philosophy

My goal is to bridge the gap between academic learning and industry practice. I believe students learn best through hands-on projects, collaboration, version control workflows, and exposure to real-world technical challenges.
